How these are made

The math is computed

Pie wedges and bar models derive their geometry from the part count, so thirds are exactly 120°. That is why a half and three sixths shade to identical widths on screen.

Strokes draw themselves

Setting pathLength=1 on an SVG shape normalises it, so a 0→1 value maps straight onto how much of the stroke has been laid down. No path measuring, works on any shape.

One source, many cuts

A pace context retimes a whole scene without touching a single keyframe. The 60-second narrated cut and the 41-second silent cut share every component.
